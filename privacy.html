Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Trivia Camp (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy and your child’s privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the Trivia Camp mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Password

Child Profiles

  • Child first name
  • Year of birth to determine question difficulty
  • Interest used to personalize trivia round

Gameplay & Progress

  • Trivia rounds played
  • Scores and progress
  • Badges earned

Voice & Audio Features

  • Text-to-speech narration
  • Optional speech recognition
  • Audio is processed in real time and not stored
  • Audio features can be turned on or off in Settings

Subscriptions

  • Handled entirely by Apple App Store
  • We do not store payment information
  • We receive subscription status only (active / expired)

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and personalize the app experience
  • Track progress and rewards
  • Manage subscriptions and access
  • Improve reliability and performance

3. Children’s Privacy

  • Accounts are created by parents or guardians
  • No direct data collection from children
  • No advertising or behavioral tracking

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Securely stored using AWS services
  • Industry-standard access controls
  • Retained only as long as necessary

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal data. Information is shared only with essential service providers or when required by law.

6. Your Choices

  • Enable or disable audio features
  • Manage subscriptions through Apple
  • Request account and data deletion

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be reflected by an updated date above.
