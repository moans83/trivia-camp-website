Privacy Policy

We care deeply about kids’ privacy and keeping things simple and safe.

Overview

Trivia Camp is designed for families and early learners. We collect only the information needed to make the app work and to improve the experience. We never sell personal data.

Information We Collect

  • Email address (for account access and support)
  • Player profiles (names, age range, progress, badges)
  • Subscription status and purchase information (handled by Apple)
  • Anonymous usage data to improve app quality

Children’s Privacy

Trivia Camp is built for kids, but accounts are managed by parents or guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal contact information from children.

Audio & Microphone

Audio features (like spoken questions) are optional. If enabled, audio is generated in real time and is not stored or reused. Microphone access is used only during gameplay and never recorded.

Subscriptions

Payments and subscription management are handled securely by Apple via the App Store. Trivia Camp does not store payment details.

Data Storage & Security

Data is stored securely using industry-standard cloud services. We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access.

Your Choices

  • You can delete your account at any time
  • You can manage subscriptions through Apple
  • You can disable audio features in settings

Contact

Questions or concerns? Reach us at support@triviacamp.app
